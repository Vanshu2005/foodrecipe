# 🍽️ Food Recipe Web

Welcome to **Food Recipe Web** — your go-to place for discovering the **master recipe** of any dish! 👨‍🍳👩‍🍳

No need to wait in long lines at cafés or restaurants! ⏳  
Just cook your **own delicious dish at home** with ease! 🏠🍛

---

## ✨ Features
- 🔍 **Search Any Dish** by name.
- 📋 **Get Step-by-Step Instructions** instantly.
- 🖼️ **See Images** of your favorite meals.
- 🌍 Explore from a wide variety of cuisines.

---

## 🚀 How to Use

1. 🧠 Think of the dish you want to cook.
2. ⌨️ Type its name in the **search bar**.
3. 🔍 Click on the **Search** button (or press Enter).
4. 📖 Get the **recipe, ingredients, and image** of the dish.
5. 🍳 Start cooking and enjoy your meal!

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- ⚛️ **React.js**
- 🌐 [TheMealDB API]

---

## 💡 Example

> Want to cook *Pasta*? 🍝  
Just type `Pasta` and hit Search.  
You'll get all the steps, ingredients, and tips to make it perfect!
